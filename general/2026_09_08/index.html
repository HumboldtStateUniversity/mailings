The Fall 2026 edition of Humboldt Magazine is now live online!
In this issue: Explore the power of access and mentorship, groundbreaking research, discoveries of the unknown, and, of course, get to know our amazing alumni.
The Making of a President: The compelling origin story of President Richard Carvajal, the lessons he learned, and the mentors who helped shape who he is today.
40 Years of Calypso Joy: Celebrate four decades of music, community, and the campus tradition that is the Calypso band.
Klamath River in Recovery: Four dams are gone, salmon are returning, and a river is finding its way forward. Faculty and students are there to research what happens next.
Dark Matter Mystery: Students and faculty work together to build a supercomputer from scratch to test how the invisible form of matter influences galaxies.
Humboldt Spirit Hits the Road: Travel with our mobile alumni center and see photos from its visits with alumni along the West Coast.
Powerful Humboldt Connection: The University’s newest mentorship program pairs students and alumni in a speed-networking-style event that builds relationships and provides support.
Plus: Take a tour of the recently renovated Jenkins Hall, explore the evolution of campus over the past two decades, and learn how alumni-funded scholarships are transforming lives.
