Fall 2026 Commencement Important Information

Fall 2026 Commencement
Friday, December 18 | Lumberjack Arena
Graduation is a huge milestone for our students and we’re looking forward to celebrating their achievements at Fall 2026 Commencement celebrations! Students who want to participate in the ceremony need to register by November 18. If there are any questions about registration, email grad@humboldt.edu.
CEREMONY SCHEDULE
Ceremony start times will be announced after registration closes, and all registered graduates will be notified of their assigned ceremony time. Ceremonies are likely to occur in the afternoon.
FACULTY & STAFF PARTICIPATION
Processional Participation
Faculty and staff planning to participate in the processional and be seated in the designated faculty and staff section during a Commencement ceremony must register their processional participation by November 18.
Faculty and staff needing to order regalia can do so with Herff Jones Faculty Direct. The deadline to order regalia to be received in time for the event is October 23 at 8:59 p.m. PST and will be available to pick up at The Campus Store around December 1.
Contact The Campus Store at (707) 826-3741 with questions regarding regalia orders.
Employee Ceremony Support
Administrators, faculty, staff, student assistants (and even volunteers) are needed to assist at Commencement behind the scenes. Roles include greeting guests, ushering, providing shuttle and wheelchair escort support, assisting with parking and traffic, and helping in the graduate and faculty/staff lineup areas.
Those who are able to work Commencement are asked to make arrangements with their supervisor for their work hours and indicate their availability and role preference by November 18. Details about shift needs and final assignments will be shared closer to the event.

VENUE ENTRY PROCEDURES
To help make Commencement Day smooth and stress-free for everyone, the University has clear bag and entry guidelines designed to enhance safety for graduates, families, and guests. Before heading to the venue, take a quick look at what not to bring.
Clear Bag Guidelines
Bags larger than 8.5” x 6” must be completely clear (like a see-through plastic, vinyl, or PVC bag). Prohibited oversized bags include, but are not limited to:
  • Backpacks
  • Tote bags
  • Large purses
  • Camera bags
Small opaque wallets or clutches smaller than 8.5” x 6” are permitted. Accommodations will be made for medically necessary items, diaper bags, and strollers. These items will be subject to inspection.
Please note: There is no secure storage for graduate or guest items.
Gown Checks for Grads and Participating Processional Faculty & Staff
There will be several security checkpoints upon arrival to Lumberjack Arena and the graduate lineup area in the West Gym (Recreation & Wellness Center, 202). Please be prepared for the following:
  • Arrive at least 45 minutes before the ceremony starts so there is plenty of time to go through security, check in, and get settled.
  • Upon arrival, there will be a quick no-touch visual search to make sure no prohibited items are being brought in. All entering the venue will be subject to the clear bag check guidelines listed above.
  • Participating graduates, faculty, and staff will need to carry their regalia through the security checkpoint. Gowns can be put on once inside and there will be mirrors available to help make final adjustments before the ceremony.
See the FAQ for more information about clear bags, entry procedures, and prohibited items.
