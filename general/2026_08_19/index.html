Get ready to show your Green & Gold spirit for Lumberjack Weekend! October 8–11
Lumberjack Weekend brings together students, staff, faculty, alumni, families, and friends for four days of events celebrating our Lumberjack community.
All participants need to register: Students, staff, and faculty have the option to register for free or for a fee with a limited-edition Lumberjack Weekend clear crossbody bag!
Weekend Highlights
There’s something happening all weekend long!
Activities kick off Thursday night with the Distinguished Alumni Awards Dinner. On Friday, shop locally made art, jewelry, ceramics, and more at the Fall Maker’s Market on the University Quad, and enjoy an evening of fabulous looks and unforgettable entertainment at the annual Lumberjack Drag Show.
Start your Saturday by cheering on the Jacks at the volleyball game before heading to the Lumberjack Weekend Block Party. Wrap up the weekend Sunday morning with a Forever Humboldt Coastal Dunes Hike.
Lumberjack Weekend Block Party
Saturday, Oct. 10 | 4–9 p.m.
Corner of B and Rossow Streets
This year’s Block Party is headlined by Durand Jones & The Indications, who will bring their signature mix of timeless soul and modern groove to Humboldt. They’ll be joined by Thee Sinseers, The Altons, and HISpanic! at the Disco for an evening packed with soul and vintage-inspired sounds.
Enjoy food trucks, carnival games, live music, and more while reconnecting with friends and celebrating the Humboldt community. Plus, the first 600 students to arrive get a free food truck meal coupon.
All attendees must register for Lumberjack Weekend to access the Block Party, so sign up early.
Come for the music, stay for the festivities, it’s a party you won’t want to miss!
