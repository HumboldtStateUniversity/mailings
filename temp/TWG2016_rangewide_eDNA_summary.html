About the data

Where samples were collected

Each marker is a lagoon or estuary sampled in summer 2016; colour is the coastal region (the recovery units used to organise the 2016 survey). Click a marker for the sample count and the number of fish taxa detected.
Interactive map needs an internet connection for base tiles.

Where species were detected

Species by site. Sites run north to south within each recovery unit. Each site has one to six water samples; the default colour is the share of a site's samples in which the species was detected. Choose Detected / not detected for a plain presence–absence matrix, and sort species by latitudinal midpoint to see the north–south turnover in composition.

Latitudinal patterns

Fish richness along the coast

Sites from north (left) to south (right). Filled circles: taxa detected at the site (all samples pooled; circle size = number of samples); open circles: mean taxa per water sample. Dashed line: linear trend of site richness.

Community composition: PCoA ordination

Principal coordinates of Jaccard distances between sites (presence–absence of all fish taxa). Sites close together share more species; colour runs from north (blue) to south (orange).
